# Corporate AI Governance & Acceptable Use Framework

**Student:** George-Cosmin NITU  
**Program:** CMDS 2025-2027  
**Current Phase:** Year 1, Semester 2 (June 2026)

## 1. Project Overview
This project aims to develop a functional "AI Compliance Toolkit" designed to help organizations manage the security, legal, and operational risks associated with Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini). It specifically addresses the rise of "Shadow AI" and the critical risk of sensitive corporate data leakage into public AI models, moving beyond total bans to provide operational, "ready-to-deploy" frameworks for IT Management.

## 2. State of the Art (SotA) Summary
* **Regulatory Landscape:** Aligned with the EU AI Act, GDPR, and the NIST AI RMF 1.0.
* **Vendor Privacy:** Incorporates technical analysis of data retention and model training policies from major providers (OpenAI, Microsoft Copilot for M365, Google Workspace).
* **The Gap:** Addressing the lack of pragmatic checklists and tiered usage policies for corporate environments.

## 3. The AI Compliance Toolkit (Proposed Architecture)
The core deliverable of this thesis is a functional prototype that supports organizational AI governance. The toolkit assists with:
* **Policy Enforcement:** Clear boundaries via an Acceptable Use Policy (AUP).
* **Compliance Documentation:** Data classification standards for Public, Internal, and Confidential inputs.
* **Risk Assessment:** Tools for vetting third-party AI vendors and maintaining a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) workflow.

## 4. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) Core Structure
1. **Purpose & Scope:** Defining the legal and technical boundaries of AI tool usage.
2. **Roles & Responsibilities:** Outlining duties for HR, IT, and end-user compliance.
3. **Data Classification Standards:** Tiered definitions dictating what data can be fed into LLMs.
4. **Approved vs. Prohibited Use Cases:** Clear guidelines on permissible operations.
5. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Workflow:** Mandates for manual review of AI-generated output for accuracy, bias, and IP infringement.
