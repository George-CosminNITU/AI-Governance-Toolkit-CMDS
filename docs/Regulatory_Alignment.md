# Regulatory Alignment Mapping

**Version:** 1.0  
**Phase:** Year 1, Semester 2 Deliverable  

## 1. Overview
This document demonstrates how the Corporate AI Governance Framework and its accompanying Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) align with the primary regulatory requirements governing Artificial Intelligence in Europe[cite: 1]. 

## 2. Alignment with the EU AI Act
The European Union’s AI Act establishes a risk-based approach to AI governance[cite: 1]. 
* **Regulatory Requirement:** The Act imposes strict transparency and governance obligations on organizations deploying AI systems[cite: 1]. Content generated by AI systems must be identifiable as such[cite: 1]. Non-compliance may result in heavy administrative fines[cite: 2].
* **Toolkit Alignment:** The framework addresses this through the AUP's mandatory "Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) workflow, ensuring transparency and accountability for AI-generated outputs[cite: 1, 2].

## 3. Alignment with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
The GDPR intersects significantly with corporate AI adoption, particularly regarding data sovereignty and automated decision-making[cite: 1].
* **Regulatory Requirement (Article 22):** GDPR Article 22 grants data subjects the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing[cite: 1].
* **Toolkit Alignment:** The AUP strictly prohibits using AI to make fully automated decisions that impact individuals (e.g., HR screening) without human intervention, effectively mitigating Article 22 risks[cite: 1]. 
* **Regulatory Requirement (Data Sovereignty):** Standard contracts with US-based AI providers often present conflicts with European data residency requirements[cite: 1, 2].
* **Toolkit Alignment:** The Data Classification Guide mandates the use of Enterprise-tier tools with strict data isolation and European data boundaries for any "Internal" data, preventing unauthorized transfers of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to public models[cite: 1, 2].

## 4. Alignment with NIST AI RMF
* **Regulatory Standard:** The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) serves as a foundational standard for risk management[cite: 1].
* **Toolkit Alignment:** The toolkit utilizes the NIST AI RMF 1.0 as the technical standard for aligning corporate risk management with global best practices[cite: 1, 2].
