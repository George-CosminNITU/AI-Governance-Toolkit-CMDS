# AI Data Classification Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** June 2026  

To prevent data leakage and intellectual property infringement when using Generative AI, all prompts and inputs must be filtered through this tiered data classification system. 

## Tier 1: Public Data
**Definition:** Information that is freely available to the public and intended for open distribution.  
**AI Usage Rule:** Safe to use with any approved AI tool, including public-tier models.  
**Examples:**
* Published marketing materials and press releases.
* Publicly available market research or academic papers.
* General industry knowledge.

## Tier 2: Internal Data
**Definition:** Information intended solely for internal company use. Unauthorized disclosure could cause minor operational disruption.  
**AI Usage Rule:** Strictly permitted **only** in Enterprise-tier AI tools that guarantee data isolation and zero-day retention (e.g., Enterprise Copilot, where inputs are not used for model training). **Banned from public/free AI models.**  
**Examples:**
* Internal memos and standard operating procedures (SOPs).
* General meeting notes (scrubbed of sensitive financial or personal data).
* Non-sensitive project plans.

## Tier 3: Confidential & Restricted Data
**Definition:** Highly sensitive information protected by law (GDPR), NDA, or corporate strategy. Unauthorized disclosure would cause severe financial, legal, or reputational damage.  
**AI Usage Rule:** **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** from all cloud-based AI tools unless a specific, secure, on-premise or highly-regulated isolated environment has been explicitly approved by the Legal and IT departments.  
**Examples:**
* Proprietary source code and intellectual property.
* Personally Identifiable Information (PII) of customers or employees (names, emails, addresses, IDs).
* Financial records, undisclosed earnings, and trade secrets.
* Strategic M&A discussions or privileged legal communications.
